ABOUT THE STUDIO

Built by brothers, sharpened by craft.

Israel Amoah Jnr and Israel Amoah Snr grew up pointing lenses at everything worth remembering. What started as two brothers borrowing a camera has grown into a full creative studio serving families, couples, brands and homeowners across Kasoa and beyond.

Today the studio brings together photography, videography, post-production and interior styling under one roof — so a client can walk in with an idea and walk out with a finished story, shot, edited and staged with the same eye.

Every booking is treated as a piece of legacy work: images and spaces that a family or a brand will still be proud of in twenty years.

WHAT WE DO

Four crafts. One studio.

Each service is led by one of the brothers directly — no handoffs, no outsourcing, just the twins and their tools.

Photography

Portraits, weddings, events and product shoots, shot with an eye for natural light and honest emotion.

Videography

Editing & Retouching

Interior Decoration
